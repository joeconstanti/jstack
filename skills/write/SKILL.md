# write

Read `.agents/jstack/rules.md` first and apply it.

When only the artifact is requested, return only that artifact. Do not append
process notes, check summaries or commentary after a requested final marker.

1. **Set the contract.** Identify reader, purpose, medium, desired action and
   supplied facts. Follow existing voice, terminology and document structure.
   Read `references/editorial.md` beside this file. Preserve approved claims
   and commitments; do not invent testimonials, prices, results or quotations.
2. **Resolve evidence gaps.** Use supplied sources for factual claims. If new
   investigation is needed, use the research skill for that question, not for
   a purely editorial rewrite. Mark a material unknown explicitly or omit the
   unsupported claim; do not make polished prose conceal uncertainty.
3. **Shape the artifact.** Follow the project's format. A proposal needs scope,
   dependencies and supplied commercial terms; a decision brief needs options,
   tradeoffs and the evidence behind the recommendation. Keep editorial notes
   outside audience-facing copy.
4. **Compare revisions** with the original for lost qualifications, changed
   facts and new obligations. Cut repetition without changing intentional terms.
5. **Check the artifact.** Validate links, numbers, dates, names, quotations and
   consistency. For PDF, slides or formatted documents, use an available
   format-specific skill/tool and inspect the rendered output. Plain Markdown
   needs a reading and structure check, not an invented build requirement.
6. **Deliver** the finished text or file, with material unresolved facts and
   validation limits.
   Writing a message does not authorize sending it or publishing the artifact.
